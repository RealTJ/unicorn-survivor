# Unicorn Survivor

This is the project for a js13k entry for the year 2026. It's a vampire survivor clone with it's own flavor. 

## Idea

The last unicorn stole all the genders. Now it's time to get it back! Defeat the minions and gather genderfluid to beat the boss back and free the genders!

### Gameplay

Like Vampire Survivor the player must traverse the map and fight against mobs. The time ticks down and at the end death comes.
The player needs to collect 8 pieces (which a permanent) to unlock the final boss.
Health and Experience is one resource (called Genderfluid). If the player is hit he loses genderfluid and needs to collect it. 
Leveling up uses up most of the genderfluid. Keeping more genderfluid than currently in the bar gets a overflow state.
